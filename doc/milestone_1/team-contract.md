## Project Scope
This contract governs collaboration for the DATA 551 dashboard project. It defines expectations, responsibilities, and working norms agreed upon by all team members. All members commit to following this agreement in good faith throughout the duration of the project.

## Work Distribution
- Tasks will be divided based on individual strengths, interests, and availability.
- Responsibilities will be clearly documented using GitHub Issues.
- Each milestone will have at least two team members reviewing deliverables before submission.
- All team members are expected to contribute meaningfully to design, implementation, and documentation.

## Working Hours and Availability
- Team members are expected to contribute approximately equal effort each week.
- Core collaboration hours are agreed to be during weekdays.
- Team members will communicate known scheduling constraints (e.g., exams, travel) in advance.
- Major deadlines will be planned with buffer time to accommodate unforeseen issues.

## Meetings and Communication
- The team will meet at least once per week, either in person or online.
- Meetings will have a brief agenda prepared in advance.
- Meeting notes and action items will be summarized in a shared document or GitHub issue.
- Communication will primarily take place via Slack, Discord, or another agreed-upon platform.

## Version Control and Collaboration
- All project work will be managed using GitHub.
- Team members will pull the latest changes before starting new work.
- Small, frequent commits with descriptive messages are encouraged.
- Pull requests will be used for significant changes, and at least one peer review is required before merging.

## Quality Standards
- Code should be readable, well-organized, and commented where necessary.
- Visualizations should follow principles of clarity, accessibility, and effective communication.
- Documentation should be clear enough that an external user can understand the project purpose and usage.

## Conflict Resolution
- Concerns or disagreements will be discussed openly and respectfully within the team.
- If consensus cannot be reached, the team will seek guidance from course instructors or teaching assistants.
- All members commit to addressing issues early to prevent escalation.

## Agreement and Amendments
- This contract represents a shared agreement among all team members.
- Updates to this contract require discussion and agreement from the entire team.
- The most recent version of this document will be kept in the project repository.

By participating in this project, all team members agree to the terms outlined in this contract.
